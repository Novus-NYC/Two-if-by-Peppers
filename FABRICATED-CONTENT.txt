FABRICATED CONTENT — Two If By Pepper
Written 2026-09-04. Everything listed here was invented to make the site read as
finished. None of it was supplied by you, and none of it is on the label art.
Nothing on the live site is marked as placeholder, so use this file as the
checklist when you swap in real details.

Almost all of it lives in one file: src/lib/brand.ts


================================================================================
1. PRODUCT FACTS  (src/lib/brand.ts — `facts`)
================================================================================

  Bottled       5 fl oz
  Batch         Under 200

All three are guesses. The bottle size and batch number in particular are the
kind of thing a customer will take literally.

2. HEAT RATINGS  (src/lib/brand.ts — `heat`, `heatName`)
================================================================================

ledger comparison table.

  Tom Sage's Lantern Fuel    4 of 5    "A long, slow burn"
  Bonney Mango               3 of 5    "Bright and immediate"

Invented. There is no Scoville figure anywhere on the site — I deliberately did

3. INGREDIENTS AND PAIRINGS  (src/lib/brand.ts — `notes`, `muster`, `build`)
================================================================================
These read as an ingredient list. They are not one.

  LANTERN FUEL
    Tasting notes   Smoked red chile, Charred garlic, Molasses,
                    Cracked black pepper, Cider vinegar
    Muster with     Brisket and burnt ends, Black beans and rice,
                    Roast potatoes, A fried egg at dawn
    Build           "Peppers roasted over open flame, then held with molasses
                    and cider until the heat stops shouting and starts
                    smouldering."

  BONNEY MANGO
    Tasting notes   Ripe mango, Habanero, Lime, Fresh ginger, Sea salt
    Muster with     Fish tacos, Grilled chicken, Slow pork shoulder,
                    A sharp aged cheddar
    Build           "Fruit first, heat second. The mango arrives before the
                    habanero does, which is the only warning you get."

Mango and habanero for Bonney are inferred from the name and the label painting.
Everything else is invented. Replace before anyone with an allergy reads it.

Full paragraphs on the /sauces page.

================================================================================
6. THE GAZETTE  (src/lib/brand.ts — `gazette`)
================================================================================
The whole /story page. Three chapters (The Spark / The Signal / The Rebellion),
expanded from the three short paragraphs on the old site, plus:

  Dateline    "Boston — the eighteenth of April, 1775"
  Masthead    "Two Lanterns Over the Harbour"
  Strapline   "printed for the flavour faithful — price, one pepper"

The 18 April 1775 date is Revere's ride, chosen to fit the "one if by land"
line. It is a deliberate historical wink, not a claim about the business.

  I    Of Small Batches   every batch small enough for one person to taste the
                          whole run

================================================================================
10. HEADLINE AND SECTION COPY  (throughout src/app/)
================================================================================
All marketing copy is new: "One if by land. Two if by sea. Two if by pepper.",
"Blandness had it coming.", "Choose your co-conspirator", "Two sauces. Two
entirely different arguments.", "The signal is still lit", "Put flavour on the
front line", "Write to the kitchen", and so on.

Note: spelling is mixed. Headline copy uses British forms in places (flavour,
harbour, smoulders, apologises) because it suits the period voice, while the
brand's own motto uses American "Fueled by Flavor". If you want one or the
other throughout, say so and I will make it consistent.

ONE THING TO CHECK ON YOUR END
  Tom Sage's label reads "Ignite Your Pallet". That is very likely meant to be
  "Palate" — a pallet is a shipping skid. I left the line off the site entirely
  rather than reproduce a probable typo or silently correct your label. Worth a
  look before the next print run.
